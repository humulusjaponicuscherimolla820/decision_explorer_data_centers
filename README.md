## 🖥️ What this app does

decision_explorer_data_centers helps you compare data center siting choices with clear rules and fixed limits. It gives you a way to look at tradeoffs such as land use, access to power, water use, grid load, transport access, and local policy limits.

The app is built for people who need a structured way to review sites and make consistent decisions. It keeps the process simple by showing the same kinds of checks for each option. That makes it easier to compare one place with another.

## 🧭 How to use it

When the app opens, you can move through a simple decision flow:

1. Choose the site or area you want to review.
2. Set the limits that matter to you, such as:
   - water use
   - power access
   - distance to roads
   - land cost
   - zoning or policy limits
3. Review each site against the same rules.
4. Compare the results side by side.
5. Save your review if you want to use it later.

The app focuses on consistency. It helps you see which sites fit the rules and which ones fail early checks.
